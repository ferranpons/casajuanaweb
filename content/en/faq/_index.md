# Frequently Asked Questions

## Where is Casa Juana?

In the **historic quarter of Cehegín** (Murcia), at Calle Clavijo 5. 10 minutes by car from Caravaca de la Cruz and 50 minutes from Murcia city.

## How many guests can stay?

The house has 3 bedrooms and accommodates **up to 6 guests**. It works well for couples, families and friend groups alike.

## Is there air conditioning?

Yes, **air conditioning is available on every floor** for maximum comfort in summer.

## Is there WiFi?

Yes, **free high-speed WiFi** throughout the house.

## Where can I park?

**Free on-street parking** is available very close to the house and is usually easy to find.

## Is it close to Caravaca de la Cruz?

Yes, Caravaca de la Cruz — a Holy City and UNESCO World Heritage site — is just **10 minutes away by car**.

## Can I book via WhatsApp?

Yes. Contact us directly on **[+34 617 448 105](https://wa.me/34617448105)** to check availability, make a booking or ask any question.

## What is the cancellation policy?

Free cancellation is available up to 5 days before arrival on most rates. Check the exact conditions when booking.

## How does check-in work?

We send you the access details in advance. Arrival is flexible — if you need a late check-in just let us know via WhatsApp ahead of time.

## Are pets allowed?

We don't accept pets at the moment. If you have a special situation, feel free to ask us directly.

## Are bed linen and towels provided?

Yes, the house includes bed linen, towels, and basic toiletries. Just arrive and relax.

## Can I book for a long stay?

Absolutely. For stays of more than 7 nights, contact us directly for the best rate.

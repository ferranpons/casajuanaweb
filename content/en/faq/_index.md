# Frequently Asked Questions

## How many guests can stay?

The house has 3 bedrooms and accommodates **up to 8 guests**. It works well for couples, families and friend groups alike.

## How does check-in work?

We send you the access details in advance. Arrival is flexible — if you need a late check-in just let us know via WhatsApp ahead of time.

## Is there parking?

Yes, free street parking is available very close to the house and is usually easy to find.

## Are pets allowed?

We don't accept pets at the moment. If you have a special situation, feel free to ask us directly.

## What is the cancellation policy?

Free cancellation is available up to 5 days before arrival on most rates. Check the exact conditions when booking.

## Are bed linen and towels provided?

Yes, the house includes bed linen, towels, and basic toiletries. Just arrive and relax.

## What is nearby?

You are in the heart of **Cehegín's old town**. Restaurants, bars, the market and the main monuments are all within a 5-minute walk. Caravaca de la Cruz (UNESCO World Heritage) is 10 minutes by car. Murcia city is 50 minutes away.

## How can I contact the hosts?

The fastest way is **WhatsApp: [+34 617 448 105](https://wa.me/34617448105)**. You can also email us at **[info@casajuanacehegin.com](mailto:info@casajuanacehegin.com)** or message us through Booking.com.

## Can I book for a long stay?

Absolutely. For stays of more than 7 nights, contact us directly for the best rate.
